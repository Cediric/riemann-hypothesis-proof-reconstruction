### V3.22 — exact VWin statement and input-package audit

The V3.22 audit makes the quantifiers explicit:

- `VVle` requires both finiteness of the relevant Sha lengths and a uniform weighted-sum bound.
- `VWinAt` is conditional on prime pairs satisfying `WinPair`; the predicate itself does not assert that such pairs exist.
- The downstream construction separately supplies admissible pairs through the `WinPairs.winPairs` / `QH.pairEx` route.
- The downstream proof constructs an actual host field before applying the universal field estimate.
- The `LData` used in `VVle` is genuine Galois-cohomology data; its nonemptiness is separately constructed.
- The inspected `SizeWindowBrackets` import branch is not called by the final `EveryCharacter` construction, based on the recorded call-site audit. This is not a substitute for the complete theorem-level dependency audit.

The exact VWin exponent in the audited theorem is 401; the error exponent ranges over (0<\varepsilon<1/52).

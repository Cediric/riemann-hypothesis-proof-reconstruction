# V3.54 — Two-copy descent and Frobenius amplification

Pinned upstream commit: 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4. Continues V3.52–53. Natural-language alternative, not a new Lean kernel run.

## 1. Exact amplification lemma

Let R be a commutative ring of characteristic p (p prime; the zero ring is trivial), u a nonzerodivisor, and D a finite tuple satisfying u^h D_j = Σ_i D_i^p c_ij. Put J_k=(D_i^(p^k):i). Then

    u^(a_k) D_j ∈ J_k,   a_k=h(1+p+...+p^(k-1))=h(p^k−1)/(p−1).

For k=0 this is tautological. Raising the kth relation to the pth power gives u^(p a_k) D_i^p ∈ J_(k+1); multiply the original relation by u^(p a_k). This gives a_(k+1)=h+p a_k. No coefficient matrices need to be constructed.

Suppose for each i and each integer M≥0 there is C_i≥0 such that u^(Mn) divides u^C_i D_i^n for all n≥0. In fact only one integer M>h/(p−1) is needed. Take C=max_i C_i (empty tuple trivial), and n=p^k. Then u^(M p^k) divides u^(C+a_k) D_j. Since u is regular, for any prescribed m choose k with M p^k−C−a_k≥m, and cancel u^(C+a_k). Thus u^m divides D_j.

This strengthens the sufficient growth threshold in phiEq_dvd_all_pow: the source takes M=h+1 and C=Σ C_i. The exact recurrence permits any integer M>h/(p−1), and the maximum replaces the sum. No claim that the threshold is necessary is made.

## 2. Jacobson localization without geometric sums

Retain the hypotheses of PointsKey.key: p=0 in R, u regular, the displayed Frobenius equation, and every map f:R→O to a perfect characteristic-p valuation domain with f(u) nonzero, nonunit and O complete for (f(u)) kills every D_j.

Let S=1+uR (equivalently 1−uR), a multiplicative set, and R'=S^(-1)R. The image of u is regular by exactness of localization. For r'=a/s, 1−u r'=(s−ua)/s is a unit because both numerator and denominator belong to S. Thus the image of u lies in the Jacobson radical. The point-vanishing hypothesis passes to R' by composition. Apply the exact upstream theorem pow_dvd_of_vanishing_at_perfect_valuation_points to each D_j in R', with M chosen as in Section 1. This theorem supplies the uniform-in-n exponent C_j; it is retained as a deep input, not inferred from ordinary prime-ideal vanishing.

Section 1 now gives D_j∈u^m R' for every m. But R'/u^m R'≅S^(-1)(R/u^m R)≅R/u^m R: every s∈S is already invertible in the quotient, because s−1 is nilpotent there. Hence D_j∈u^m R. This proves the exact PointsKey.key conclusion without clearing a second denominator or writing a geometric series. The case m=0 is automatic.

## 3. Tensor descent via a linear retraction

Let A→B be a flat commutative algebra, p=0 in B, u∈A regular on B, and B separated for (u). Let pr:B→A be A-linear with pr(1)=1. Let x_j∈B be a finite tuple satisfying both universal hypotheses of descent_of_points_killed:

(i) for every commutative T and two maps B→T agreeing on A, with the common image of u a unit, the differences Δ_j satisfy Δ_j=Σ_i Δ_i^p c_ij for some c_ij∈T;

(ii) for every pair of maps B→O agreeing on A, where O is a perfect characteristic-p valuation domain, the common image of u is nonzero and nonunit and O is complete for its principal ideal, the images of x_j agree.

Set R=B⊗_A B, U=u·1, D_j=x_j⊗1−1⊗x_j. Flatness of B over A preserves injectivity of multiplication by u on B after tensoring with B, so U is regular. Apply (i) to the canonical maps into R[1/U]. Finiteness of the tuple gives a single denominator U^h for all coefficients. Injectivity of R→R[1/U] then gives U^h D_j=Σ_i D_i^p c'_ij in R. Any admissible valuation point of R gives two admissible maps of B agreeing on A, hence kills D_j by (ii). Section 2 gives D_j∈U^mR for all m.

Apply the A-linear map L:R→B defined by L(b⊗b')=pr(b)b'. Then L(D_j)=pr(x_j)·1−x_j and L(U^mR)⊂u^mB. Separatedness forces L(D_j)=0, so x_j=pr(x_j)·1. Notice that R itself need not be separated and pr need not be multiplicative. This proves the exact descent_of_points_killed conclusion.

## 4. Scope

The two-copy tensor argument and Frobenius amplification are now reconstructed, with the sharper growth threshold and a quotient-localization replacement for explicit denominator algebra. The remaining valuation-theoretic theorem pow_dvd_of_vanishing_at_perfect_valuation_points and the geometric comparison exists_fixFlat_of_tilt_maps_agree remain essential. In particular this is not yet an independent proof of the tilt descent principle of V3.52, nor of RH/GRH.

# V3.29 — H9 upstream compression: reduce the family hypotheses

> Upstream snapshot: CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean at commit 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4.
>
> Scope: reduce the family-level premises of the corrected-lattice slope bound by following the actual constructors. This is a dependency compression, not a proof of the deepest arithmetic inputs.

## 1. The apparent six-input statement

V3.28 established the formal implication

\[
\text{LevelTFamily}+\text{NuCommensF}+\text{TuSize}
+\text{MuRank}+\text{LinkG}+\text{LinkMu}
\Longrightarrow \text{PlacesF}
\Longrightarrow \text{SlopesNF}.
\]

The first two terms are not independent assumptions. Both are consequences of the same family-level level package LvlF.

## 2. LvlF is the real level entry point

In LinkMin.lean,

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{LvlF}(Fm)\;:\Longleftrightarrow\;&
\exists x_0,\ \forall x\geq x_0,\ \forall i\in Fm.Ix(x),\\
&\left(\forall u,\ \text{family\_property\_and}(D_{x,i},u)\right)
\land
\left(\forall u,\ \text{CongruenceLatticeLengthBound}(D_{x,i},u)\right).
\end{aligned}
\]

It is assembled by lvlF_of from three family statements:

1. SettingEventually;
2. the eventual family property family_property_and;
3. the eventual CongruenceLatticeLengthBound.

The first is used to ensure the word-level Hida setting in the two latter statements. The proof merely takes the maximum of their three thresholds.

From LvlF, two of the six inputs follow:

- nuCommensF_min derives NuCommensF using NuDef and the level-at-T part of family_property_and;
- lemma_five_one_of_theorem_two derives LevelTFamily from LvlF and TuSize, using numsL_wtsL_fam, fifthLevelSizes_of_weights, and levelPrime_setting_of_sizes.

Thus neither LevelTFamily nor NuCommensF should be counted as an independent H9 hypothesis once LvlF and TuSize are available.

## 3. NuFullF and MuRank are also constructible

The same source chain gives:

\[
\text{LvlF}\Longrightarrow\text{NuCommensF}
\Longrightarrow\text{NuFullF}.
\]

The first arrow is nuCommensF_min; the second is nuFullF_of_lvl in LvlWire.lean.

The positivity of the corrected rank is supplied by muRank_min in LinkMin.lean. Its actual inputs are:

- C0 > 0;
- ChosenProviso;
- NuFullF;
- LvlF;
- SettingEventually.

The proof identifies the corrected rank with the arithmetic dimension du, uses the level-prime identity to show the relevant factor \(1-1/(T-1)\) is positive, and invokes the positive duS bound from ChosenProviso.

Therefore MuRank is not an independent input if the above level, full-rank, and proviso data have already been constructed.

## 4. LinkMu can be derived from size and rank data

The definition is

\[
\text{LinkMu}(Fm)\;:\Longleftrightarrow\;
\exists C,x_0,\ \forall x\geq x_0,\ \forall i,\ 
\text{NprimeMuR}(L_{x,i},C),
\]

where

\[
\left|
\mu(N_u)-\mu(NuH_u)
-\sum_{w\in\operatorname{supp}}sz(w,sg(u,w))
\right|
\leq C\,X''_n.
\]

This is the comparison between the corrected-lattice slope, the auxiliary NuH slope, and the local gain sum.

There are two source constructors:

- linkMu_of_sizes derives LinkMu from TuSize, DGamSize, MuFG, and MuRank;
- linkMu_of_full derives it from TuSize, DGamSize, NuFullF, and MuRank.

The second is the cleaner route after NuFullF has already been obtained from LvlF. In particular, LinkMu is not an independent premise once the full-rank, size, degree, and rank inputs are present.

## 5. The corrected-lattice half of H9 with redundant inputs removed

Substitute the preceding constructors into placesF_min' and then slopesNF_of. A compact sufficient input list is:

\[
\boxed{
\begin{gathered}
\text{SettingEventually},\
\text{FamU}(\text{family\_property\_and}),\
\text{FamU}(\text{CongruenceLatticeLengthBound}),\\
\text{TuSize},\ \text{DGamSize},\ \text{ChosenProviso},\ \text{LinkG},\
C_0>0.
\end{gathered}
}
\]

These imply LvlF, NuCommensF, LevelTFamily, NuFullF, MuRank, and LinkMu, and hence PlacesF and SlopesNF.

This is the reduced interface. It is still conditional on LinkG and the family construction inputs; it is not a claim that those premises are trivial.

## 6. LinkG is itself a constructed package

The theorem linkG_fam_noKer in AdmissibleFamilyNoKernel.lean supplies LinkG from a more explicit list:

- SecondTheoremAtFamily and FirstTheoremAtFamily;
- positivity of the global constant La and the lower bound on Ce;
- eventual CbLarge;
- TrivialStabiliserFiniteClasses;
- J.StdSubAt;
- Adm2F and NPDescF;
- CornerGLContinuity.FamilyWindowLogBound.

LinkG_at then contains the local divisibility inclusion and the local length estimate used by places_at_of. This is the next upstream branch to audit; the present document does not flatten those premises into a single opaque assumption.

## 7. The resulting dependency tree

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{SettingEventually}+
\text{FamU(property)}+
\text{FamU(congruence length)}
\Longrightarrow \text{LvlF},\\
&\text{LvlF}\Longrightarrow
\text{NuCommensF},\ \text{NuFullF},\\
&\text{LvlF}+\text{TuSize}
\Longrightarrow\text{LevelTFamily},\\
&\text{LvlF}+\text{NuFullF}+\text{ChosenProviso}
+\text{SettingEventually}
\Longrightarrow\text{MuRank},\\
&\text{NuFullF}+\text{MuRank}+\text{TuSize}+\text{DGamSize}
\Longrightarrow\text{LinkMu},\\
&\text{LevelTFamily}+\text{NuCommensF}+\text{TuSize}
+\text{MuRank}+\text{LinkG}+\text{LinkMu}
\Longrightarrow\text{PlacesF}
\Longrightarrow\text{SlopesNF}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The only uncompressed branch in this diagram is LinkG and the construction of the family data that feed LvlF and ChosenProviso.

## 8. What this changes in the human proof

The human exposition treats H9 as an assumed range-of-slopes statement. The Lean source proves the corrected-lattice half from a much more explicit set of family-level conditions. After removing derived interfaces, the dependency list is shorter and more meaningful:

- level and congruence-lattice data;
- size and degree control;
- a positive-rank proviso;
- the local divisibility package LinkG.

The denominator/slope comparison LinkMu is then derived, rather than independently assumed. This is a genuine simplification of the proof dependency graph.

The remaining hard work is to prove the family-level inputs and trace LinkG back to the construction. H5, the first-lattice covolume, and H12, the uniform scale-window supply, remain separate bottlenecks for the full arithmetic inequality (M).

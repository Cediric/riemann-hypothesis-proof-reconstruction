## 1. The final theorem is not conditional on VWin

The intermediate theorem zero_free_all_of_green_v_win has three inputs:

- Green;
- VWin;
- the definite-side HypsAlt package, including the closed-family LinkN condition.

It proves ZeroFreeAll. By itself, it is conditional.

But MainConclusion.lean does not leave these inputs open. It defines

\[
\text{everyCharacter\_of\_row}(h)
\]

from a Row0b proof h by:

1. constructing OpenLiteratureStatements as the tuple \(\langle h,\ recTExists,\ h\rangle\);
2. deriving VWin using vwin_of_row h;
3. applying every_character_of_literature_of_vWin.

It then defines

\[
\text{everyCharacter}
=
\text{everyCharacter\_of\_row}
\bigl(\text{galoisLocalGlobalStrong\_recParameter}\bigr).
\]

The main RH theorem in Main.lean is obtained from this EveryCharacter theorem. Therefore the final Lean theorem has no explicit VWin, Green, or HypsAlt parameters. Those are discharged inside the proof from Row0b and the literature/definite-side construction.

The correct distinction is:

- zero_free_all_of_green_v_win: conditional intermediate lemma;
- everyCharacter and the final RiemannHypothesis theorem: closed theorem statements, with the intermediate inputs constructed internally.

## 3. What TLiftK asserts

TLiftK is a universal theta-lift existence statement. For every CM number field E, every admissible base B, every Hermitian source space X, every sufficiently large skew target space W, compatible Hecke characters and exponent data, and every unitary automorphic representation U with the required weights, it produces a target representation U' and an explicit theta-lift relation

\[
\operatorname{IsLift}(B,\chi_X,\chi_W,m_X,m_W,\lambda,U,U').
\]

This is a substantial automorphic representation theorem, not a small algebraic lemma. It is the central proof engine feeding the Galois local-global statement.

## 4. How TLiftK is proved

TPGHolds.lean proves

\[
\text{tLift\_holds\_k}:\text{TLiftK}
\]

by applying tLift_of_tools_k to Tools_k and HgtEx_k.

The two packages are constructed as follows.

### A. Tools_k

tools_holds_k constructs Tools_k from:

- anisotropic Siegel–Weil and standard Siegel–Weil inputs;
- frame conjugation and finite-level results;
- compactness/stabilizer data;
- Eisenstein continuation;
- box-section estimates;
- the double/unfolding and polynomial-Schur identities;
- theta-basic and pair-existence results;
- free splitting and vacuum fixing;
- the group action and Lie-algebra first/second derivative identities.

The source constructs intermediate objects p1, l1, and l2, then fills every field of Tools_k. In particular:

- siegelWeilAniso_of_k builds the anisotropic Siegel–Weil package;
- boxSecX_of_k supplies the box-section estimate;
- lieSecond2 supplies the second Lie identity;
- swFullX_of_k combines the Siegel–Weil and Eisenstein-continuation inputs;
- cptAll_of_k supplies compactness;
- pairEx_of_k combines model existence and Weil existence.

### B. HgtEx_k

The height-existence input is obtained from

\[
\text{hgtEx\_of\_k}(\text{stab2Cpt\_k}).
\]

It is not an additional external parameter to tLift_holds_k.

### C. Assemble the theta lift

tLift_of_tools_k then combines:

- branch existence;
- the first-height estimate;
- the real-pair theorem;
- nonvanishing, built from Rallis, decomposition, bump, datum, tail, absolute-nonzero, and Schur-step results;
- automorphy of the lift;
- projection existence;
- the read-lift theorem.

The output is TLiftK.

The proof is a composition of already-proved representation-theoretic lemmas, but the universal statement TLiftK is the main mathematical content of this branch.

## 5. The corrected end-to-end theorem chain

The full final proof can now be represented as:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{Tools}_k+\text{HgtEx}_k
\Longrightarrow \text{TLiftK},\\
&\text{GeoOne}+\text{TLiftK}
\Longrightarrow \text{Row0b},\\
&\text{Row0b}
\Longrightarrow \text{VWin}+\text{OpenLiteratureStatements},\\
&\text{VWin}+\text{Green}+\text{HypsAlt}

&\text{ZeroFreeAll}

The Green and definite-side packages are assembled in the literature theorem wrapper from Row0b and the associated row statements. They are not free-standing assumptions of the final theorem.

The previous H12 analysis remains valid, but its role is now correctly understood:

- H12's windowed family is constructed from VWinAt and the recursion/object branch;

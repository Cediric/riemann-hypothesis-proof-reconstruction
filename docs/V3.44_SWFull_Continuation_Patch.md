# V3.44 — SWFull compressed: analytic continuation patched by the anisotropic Siegel–Weil value

> Scope: reconstruct the proof of the full Siegel–Weil package SWFull from the three inputs SWAniso, EisContinues, and BoxSec. This is the exact special-value bridge used by the Rallis argument.

## 1. What SWFull asserts

For the relevant good base, Hermitian source X, skew target W, model M, Hecke character chi, doubling datum, height a, compact stabilizer K, and Haar fundamental domain D, SWFull supplies:

- an open set U in the complex parameter plane;
- a meromorphic continuation Ê of the flat Eisenstein section;
- regularity at the special point
  \[
  s_0=\frac{d-n}{2};
  \]
- the special-value identity
  \[
  \widehat E(s_0,x)
  =
  c_{n,d}\int_D
  \thetaSum\bigl(\omega(x,S(h)\Phi)\bigr)\,d\mu(h),
  \]
  where \(c_{n,d}=1\) if n<d and \(2\) otherwise.

In the Lean source this is the SWFullX package in BoxSecX.lean.

## 2. The three inputs

### SWAniso

SWAniso gives the value of the Eisenstein series at s0 in the anisotropic case. Its output is an EisValueAt statement: the continued Eisenstein series is regular at s0 and its value equals the theta average over the chosen fundamental domain.

### EisContinues

EisContinues supplies a meromorphic continuation Ê on an open set U, together with regularity information at the points where the continuation is used.

### BoxSec

BoxSec ensures that the section

\[
x\longmapsto l\bigl(\omega(x,\operatorname{boxFun}(\Phi_1,\overline{\Phi_2}))\bigr)
\]

belongs to the finite-dimensional section space required by the Eisenstein continuation theorem. This is where the finite-level and stabilizer-compactness inputs enter.

## 3. The proof swFull_of is a continuation patch

The theorem swFull_of has the short proof:

1. apply EisContinues to obtain U, Ê, the continuation proof, and regularity at s0;
2. apply BoxSec to show the chosen box section is admissible;
3. apply SWAniso to obtain EisValueAt at s0;
4. use IsEisCont.update to replace the value of Ê at s0 by the anisotropic theta-average value.

The key analytic lemma IsEisCont.update says that if Ê is meromorphic/holomorphic on U, regular at s0, and a prescribed value is known at s0, then the continuation can be extended to insert s0 into U with that value. No new analytic continuation is performed at this stage.

Thus:

\[
\text{EisContinues}+\text{BoxSec}+\text{SWAniso}
\Longrightarrow \text{SWFull}.
\]

The special value is supplied by SWAniso; the global continuation is supplied by EisContinues; BoxSec connects the chosen polynomial data to the continuation theorem.

## 4. How the source constructs SWAniso

ThkBaseSWStd.lean proves

\[
\text{siegelWeilAniso\_of\_k}
(\text{SiegelWeilStd}_k,\text{FrameConj}_k,\text{FinLevel}_k,\text{Stab2Cpt}_k).
\]

The central lemma sw_transl_k transports the standard Siegel–Weil formula to the required Hermitian/skew pair.

The transport works as follows:

1. FrameConj_k finds a conjugating parabolic element p, a compact subgroup K0, and a determinant factor t.
2. Put \(d=|\det t|_E>0\).
3. Replace the height a by
   \[
   a_0(x)=d\,a(xp^{-1}).
   \]
4. The height-invariance property transfers from K to K0 under conjugation.
5. Apply the standard Siegel–Weil formula to the conjugated data and the translated box section.
6. Undo the conjugation and rescale the Eisenstein parameter by the factor \(d^{s_0-z}\).

The finite-level and stabilizer-compactness hypotheses are used to prove that the translated section remains in the required finite-dimensional section space.

This is a change-of-frame argument, not a second independent Siegel–Weil theorem.

## 5. How the source constructs EisContinues

ThkBaseEisCont.lean proves

\[
\text{eisContinues\_of\_k}
(\text{EisBL}_k,\text{FrameConj}_k).
\]

It uses the same conjugation data p, K0, and d.

1. Translate the section f to \(x\mapsto f(xp^{-1})\).
2. Transfer its finite-dimensionality and K-invariance to K0.
3. Apply EisBL_k to obtain the meromorphic continuation and its regularity/growth package.
4. Multiply by the explicit factor \(d^{s_1-z}\) and translate the group variable by p.
5. Use the regularity-on-inserted-point lemma to obtain the required IsEisCont statement.

The determinant factor is explicit and positive, so its complex power is holomorphic on the parameter domain.

## 6. The full dependency chain

The proof of the full Siegel–Weil package is therefore:

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\text{SiegelWeilStd}_k+\text{FrameConj}_k
+\text{FinLevel}_k+\text{Stab2Cpt}_k\\
&\qquad\Longrightarrow \text{SWAniso},\\
&\text{EisBL}_k+\text{FrameConj}_k
\Longrightarrow \text{EisContinues},\\
&\text{FinLevel}_k+\text{Stab2Cpt}_k+\text{box identities}
\Longrightarrow \text{BoxSec},\\
&\text{SWAniso}+\text{EisContinues}+\text{BoxSec}
\Longrightarrow \text{SWFull}.
\end{aligned}
\]

This is the SWFull input used by the theta-lift Tools_k package.

## 7. What remains to audit

The continuation patch itself is now elementary once the three inputs are available. The remaining substantive inputs are:

- the standard Siegel–Weil formula SiegelWeilStd_k;
- the frame-conjugation theorem FrameConj_k;
- the finite-level and stabilizer-compactness results;
- EisBL_k, the meromorphic continuation/growth theorem for the Eisenstein family;
- the box-section identity and finite-dimensionality statement.

The next proof-reconstruction task should expand EisBL_k and the standard Siegel–Weil input. Those are the analytic foundations that the short SWFull wrapper is composing.

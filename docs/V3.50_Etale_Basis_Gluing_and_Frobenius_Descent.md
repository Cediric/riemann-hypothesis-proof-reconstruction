# V3.50 — Étale basis gluing, Frobenius unit lemma and preframe descent

Pinned upstream 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4. Continues V3.47–V3.49. This document gives condensed proofs of the étale gluing argument and final Frobenius descent, and a complete root-based argument for the uniform denominator unit lemma modulo stated standard preparation/divisibility inputs. It does not claim a fresh Lean verification or an independent proof of RH.

Notation: p=ℓ, S=W(F_q)[[X]], A=Ainf, W=W(C^flat), e:S→A and i:A→W, eW=i∘e. OE=bk.OE. Denote Frobenius on these rings by φ and all integral coefficient embeddings implicitly when multiplying matrices.

## 1. Gluing local étale bases by a gauge change

At every positive precision n, exists_etaleBasis_modPow supplies T_n∈GL_d(W) and R_n,R'_n over OE with
    Π_n F_n.T ≡ T_n R_n,
    Π_n F_n.T R'_n ≡ T_n          (mod p^n).
The second formula and the first imply R_n R'_n≡I mod p^n. The layer transition f_n gives a matrix Y_n over S satisfying f_n F_(n+1).T=F_n.T eW(Y_n) by the equivariant-map theorem. Tower compatibility Π_n f_n≡Π_(n+1) mod p^n yields
    T_(n+1) ≡ T_n U_n mod p^n,
    U_n=R_n eW(Y_n) R'_(n+1) ∈ M_d(OE).
Indeed use the second formula at n+1, replace Π_(n+1) by Π_n f_n, then use equivariance and the first formula at n.

Since both T_n,T_(n+1) are units and n≥1, U_n is invertible in W (its determinant is nonzero mod p). The OE valuation-factorization property implies U_n is invertible over OE as well.

Define C_1=I, C_(n+1)=U_n^(-1)C_n, all in GL_d(OE). Then
    T_(n+1)C_(n+1)≡T_n C_n mod p^n.
Completeness gives one T0 with T0≡T_n C_n mod p^n; its mod-p reduction is invertible, hence T0 is invertible. Replace R_n by C_n^(-1)R_n and R'_n by R'_n C_n. We now have
    Π_n F_n.T≡T0 R_n,
    R_n R'_n≡I mod p^n
simultaneously at every n≥1. At n=0 both congruences are vacuous. This is the content of EtaleLimit.main. It does not assume that the initially chosen T_n are themselves compatible. It only uses the fact that they differ modulo p^n by a change of basis over OE.

The arithmetic inputs not proved by this argument are finite-precision étale basis existence and exact equivariant-map descent over S.

## 2. Recover global invariance and Frobenius data

Write T_n^layer=F_n.T and P_n=Π_n T_n^layer. The normalized data above give, modulo p^n,
    P_n=T0 R_n,    T0=P_n R'_n.
All congruences below are in W/p^n.

For g in FixFlat, g(R'_n)=R'_n by the fixedness of OE, and the integral Π_n is fixed. Tower equivariance and the layer's fixedness yield
    ρ(g)g(T0)
      =ρ(g)Π_n g(T_n^layer)R'_n
      =Π_n ρ_n(g)g(T_n^layer)R'_n
      =P_n R'_n=T0.
Since this holds for every n, p-adic separatedness gives exact invariance.

Let B_n=eW(E)^(-N)eW(F_n.A), with inverse B'_n=eW(E)^(-N)eW(F_n.A'). These lie in GL_d_n(OE), because E is not divisible by p, so eW(E) is a unit in OE, and AA'=E^(2N) I. The preframe relation is φ(T_n^layer)=T_n^layer B_n. Then
    T0^(-1)φ(T0) ≡ R_n B_n φ(R'_n),
    φ(T0)^(-1)T0 ≡ φ(R_n) B'_n R'_n       (mod p^n).
For the first equality use φ(T0)=φ(P_n)φ(R'_n) and φ(P_n)=P_n B_n; for the second use P_n=φ(P_n)B'_n and φ(P_n)=φ(T0)φ(R_n).

Both approximants have entries in OE. Its closedness implies A0=T0^(-1)φ(T0) and A0'=φ(T0)^(-1)T0 have entries in OE; they are mutually inverse. This is the content of LimBasis.main.

After V3.48 supplies Wm∈GL_d(OE), put T=T0 Wm. Fixedness is preserved since FixFlat fixes Wm. Frobenius changes by the single gauge identity
    A1=Wm^(-1) A0 φ(Wm),     A1'=φ(Wm)^(-1) A0' Wm.
These matrices are inverse and in OE. This gives ChangeEtaleBasis.main.

## 3. A root-theoretic proof of the Frobenius unit lemma

The denominator argument in V3.49 requires the following property of the precise BK datum:
    p∤g in S,   φ_S(g)|E^M g  ⇒  g∈S^×.
The assumptions on the Eisenstein polynomial E are important: it is monic and Eisenstein over the unramified coefficient DVR, and the source's not_expand field requires at least one nonzero coefficient at an index not divisible by p.

By Weierstrass preparation write g=UP with U a power-series unit and P monic distinguished. Remove units from the divisibility and use the source's polynomial divisibility descent to get
    σ(P)(X^p) | E(X)^M P(X)
as polynomials. Here σ is coefficient Frobenius. The descent is a standard Weierstrass division consequence, but remains an explicit source input.

If P has positive degree, first P(0)≠0: otherwise the order of vanishing at X=0 on the left is p times that on the right, since E(0)≠0, which is impossible. All roots of P have absolute value <1 (distinguished polynomial). Choose one x of maximal norm r, so 0<r<1. Extend coefficient Frobenius to an isometric automorphism of the algebraic closure, and write z=σ̃(x), a root of σ(P) of norm r.

Let m_z be the minimal polynomial of z over the unramified coefficient fraction field K0. Each conjugate z' is a root of σ(P) and has norm r. Every y with y^p=z' has norm r^(1/p)>r. The divisibility above implies E(y)^M P(y)=0, but P(y)≠0 by maximality. If M=0 this is already a contradiction. Otherwise every such y is a root of E.

Now F(X)=m_z(X^p) is monic and separable: m_z is separable in characteristic zero, z≠0, and each nonzero root has p distinct p-th roots. All roots of F are roots of E. Thus F divides E over the algebraic closure, and monic division gives F|E in K0[X]. Since E is Eisenstein and hence irreducible over K0, F=E. This forces every coefficient E_i with p∤i to vanish, contradicting not_expand.

Therefore P=1 and g is a unit. This condenses LemZ.isUnit_of_frob_dvd and ExpandCoeff.coeff_eq_zero_of_roots into one root-containment argument, using the stated preparation, polynomial descent, Frobenius extension/isometry and irreducibility inputs. It does NOT claim the lemma for an arbitrary Eisenstein polynomial: not_expand is needed (the root argument would not contradict E=F(X^p)).

Consequently V3.49's saturated coefficient ideal proof now reduces to those standard inputs and topological basis existence. In particular an S-linear functional L_M:A→S with L_M(τ^M)=p^r is available from the coefficient theorem.

## 4. Period-denominator descent using the same functional

Suppose w∈OE and a∈A with i(a)=i(τ)^M w. We show w∈eW(S).

For every n, the OE definition gives m,s such that
    eW(X)^m w≡eW(s) mod p^n W.
Multiplying by i(τ)^M and reflecting p^n-divisibility through A→W yields
    e(X)^m a≡τ^M e(s) mod p^n A.
Apply the S-linear functional L_M:
    X^m L_M(a)≡p^r s mod p^n S.
Map to W, then cancel the unit eW(X)^m. It follows for every n that
    eW(L_M(a))≡p^r w mod p^n W.
Separatedness gives exact equality eW(L_M(a))=p^r w.

There is a shorter final descent than introducing a separate integral-evaluation membership theorem. The source's BK.evW_isUnit_injective_dvd reflects divisibility by p through e:S→A, and A→W reflects divisibility by p^k by Witt truncation. Therefore eW:S→W reflects divisibility by p, and by iteration by p^r (cancel nonzero p in W). Since p^r divides eW(L_M(a)) in W, write L_M(a)=p^r s0 in S. Cancellation yields w=eW(s0).

This proves KisinFF.scalar; apply entrywise to matrices to obtain KisinFF.main. It is the SAME functional as the uniform-right-inverse argument, which unifies the two apparently separate denominator descents. No assumption that τ^M itself has a coefficient in S equal to p^r is made.

## 5. Frobenius matrices descend and give the target preframe

From the adjusted basis T of Section 2 and V3.49, we have
    φ(T)=T A1, A1 A1'=I, A1,A1'∈M_d(OE),
    i(Y)=t^N T,   i(Y')=t^N T^(-1),  t=i(τ).
Use φ(t)=eW(E)t. Then
    i(Y' φ(Y))= t^(2N) eW(E)^N A1,
    i(φ(Y')Y)= t^(2N) eW(E)^N A1'.
Indeed φ(T^(-1))T=A1'. The expressions on the left are integral A-matrices. The matrices eW(E)^N A1 and eW(E)^N A1' have entries in OE. Section 4 applies to each entry with M=2N to produce A_0,A_0' over S with
    eW(A_0)=eW(E)^N A1,   eW(A_0')=eW(E)^N A1'.
Their product maps to eW(E)^(2N) I. Injectivity of eW gives A_0 A_0'=E^(2N) I, and φ(T)=T A1 yields eW(E)^N φ(T)=T eW(A_0). The invariance from Section 2 completes Pre at height N. This is pre_of_etale.

# V3.38 — A shorter trace-limit proof by uniform approximation

Pinned upstream: CocoSgt/riemann-hypothesis-lean, commit 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4.

## Scope

This note gives a direct natural-language alternative proof of the precise approximation-transfer lemma in UpperTheory.Gal.Lim.exists_trace_limit. It replaces much of the filter/product-closure bookkeeping by a uniform Cauchy argument. It is not a new proof of RH, and this alternative proof has not been formalized or kernel-checked. The original theorem is the reference for its exact statement.

Source: src/Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/Foundations/GaloisRepresentations/TraceLimits/ExistsTraceLimit.lean.

## 1. Abstract statement

Let K be the normed field of coefficients (the algebraic closure of Q_l with its l-adic norm), and L a finite-dimensional extension of Q_l inside K. Thus L is complete in its induced metric, even though K need not be complete.

Let G be a topological group, J and I index sets, x:J→G, theta:J→L, and let f_i:G→K be continuous functions, in the application traces of continuous fixed-rank representations. Let eta_i:J→K and D_i⊆J satisfy

\[
\eta_i(j)=f_i(x(j))\quad(j\in D_i),
\qquad
\overline{x(\bigcap_{i\in F}D_i)}=G
\quad(F\subset I\text{ finite}).
\]

Suppose theta is polynomially approximable by the eta_i: for each epsilon>0 there exist a finite F⊆I and coefficients c_i∈K such that, simultaneously for every integer-coefficient polynomial P in the J-indexed variables,

\[
|P(\theta)-\sum_{i\in F}c_i P(\eta_i)|\leq\varepsilon. \tag{1}
\]

Polynomials have finite support. The coefficients in (1) are independent of P. This is essential: separate approximations for individual polynomials would not establish the result. The constant polynomial is included. Repeated eta_i can be indexed by choosing one representative for each element of a finite support.

Claim: there exists t:G→L, with the same approximation inequalities transferred to all polynomials in G-indexed variables evaluated at t and f_i. In particular, any subset A⊆I that already polynomially approximates theta also polynomially approximates t, and t(x(j))=theta(j) whenever j∈D_i for every i∈A.

## 2. Construct t directly, without assuming K complete

Choose positive epsilon_n→0 and witnesses F_n,c_{ni} to (1). Write

\[
s_n(g)=\sum_{i\in F_n}c_{ni}f_i(g),\qquad
D_F=\bigcap_{i\in F} D_i .
\]

Applying (1) to the coordinate polynomial X_j gives

\[
|\theta(j)-s_n(x(j))|\leq\varepsilon_n
\quad(j\in D_{F_n}). \tag{2}
\]

On the dense set x(D_{F_n∪F_m}), the triangle inequality gives

\[
|s_n-s_m|\leq\varepsilon_n+\varepsilon_m.
\]

Continuity of the two finite sums extends this to all of G. In particular the sequence is uniformly Cauchy, but its values need not lie in L.

Fix g∈G. By density and continuity choose j_n∈D_{F_n} such that

\[
|s_n(x(j_n))-s_n(g)|<\varepsilon_n.
\]

Then |theta(j_n)-s_n(g)|≤2epsilon_n. Since theta(j_n)∈L, these values form a Cauchy sequence in the complete field L. Let t(g) be its limit. The same estimates show s_n(g)→t(g); passing m→∞ in the uniform bound above yields

\[
|t(g)-s_n(g)|\leq\varepsilon_n \quad(g\in G). \tag{3}
\]

Consequently t is continuous, being a uniform limit of continuous functions. The construction works for arbitrary G, with no first-countability or metrizability assumption on G: each j_n is chosen using continuity of the scalar function s_n, not convergence in G. Uniqueness among functions satisfying the transferred approximation bounds follows immediately from (3).

## 3. Transfer all polynomial inequalities

Fix ANY finite witness F,c,epsilon satisfying (1), not necessarily one of the chosen sequence. Fix a polynomial Q in the G-indexed variables, depending only on a finite set g_1,...,g_d.

For each n and k, use density of x(D_{F∪F_n}) and continuity of s_n and of the finitely many f_i (i∈F) to choose j_{nk} in D_{F∪F_n} with

\[
|s_n(x(j_{nk}))-s_n(g_k)|<\varepsilon_n,
\qquad
|f_i(x(j_{nk}))-f_i(g_k)|<\varepsilon_n\quad(i\in F).
\]

By (2) and (3),

\[
|\theta(j_{nk})-t(g_k)|\leq 3\varepsilon_n.
\]

Also eta_i(j_{nk})→f_i(g_k) for every i∈F. In (1), substitute the polynomial obtained from Q by replacing the finitely many variables X_{g_k} with X_{j_{nk}}. The bound holds even if some chosen indices coincide, because polynomial substitution is valid in that case too. Passing to the limit using continuity of finite-variable polynomial evaluation gives

\[
|Q(t)-\sum_{i\in F}c_i Q(f_i)|\leq\varepsilon. \tag{4}
\]

This proves the full approximation transfer. Taking Q=X_g recovers the pointwise bound for every original witness.

## 4. Recover prescribed trace values

If theta is polynomially approximable by eta_i with i∈A, choose witnesses with F⊆A in (1). Equation (4) proves polynomial approximation of t by f_i from the same subset. If j∈D_i for all i∈A, then

\[
\begin{aligned}
|t(x(j))-\theta(j)|
&\leq |t(x(j))-\sum_{i\in F}c_i f_i(x(j))|\\
&\quad+|\sum_{i\in F}c_i\eta_i(j)-\theta(j)|\\
&\leq 2\varepsilon.
\end{aligned}
\]

As epsilon is arbitrary the two values agree. When writing approximation in terms of the set of trace functions instead of the indexed family, merge coefficients for equal trace functions; the finite sum is unchanged.

## 5. Role in the Galois representation argument

In TraceLimits/LimFinOddOf.lean, limWU4_of implements the preceding lemma with f_i=tr(rho_i), eta_i the automorphic eigenvalue systems, x(j) chosen Frobenius elements, and D_i the matching loci. SeedWU4 and CongUW4 provide the seed representations and polynomial approximation. Chebotarev-type density and the finite-intersection argument supply the dense common matching loci. The congruence package supplies a single finite coefficient extension L for theta.

The next step is NOT an elementary consequence of the uniform-limit lemma: exists_limit_rep constructs a continuous semisimple representation r with trace t from polynomial approximation by traces. The source then uses linApprox_of_polyApprox_trc to propagate information on each selected subset of seeds. In particular it applies the one-place families to unramified/Frobenius information, crystalline and de Rham conditions, and joint local power-sum approximations. Those representation-existence and local-compatibility results remain dependencies; this note does not replace them.

At the higher level, limFinOdd_of specializes limWU4_of to odd rank. In IntegralStructures/Development.lean the seed and congruence branches are combined with TLiftK, ClosedPhi and DescOffT to give Core, then CoreAll and Row0. Row0.row0b restricts the local compatibility conditions to the stated big-prime regime; it does not construct new representations. This is the Row0b used by MainConclusion.

## 6. What is simplified

The abstract approximation-transfer lemma has been replaced by four short mathematical steps: dense-set comparison, convergence in L, finite-variable polynomial transfer, and exact coordinate recovery. The key guard against an invalid proof is that completeness is invoked only for L, not the algebraic closure K. No assumptions about convergence of the original representations or individual coefficients are added.

The proof is a direct reorganization of the formal result, not a claim of new mathematical novelty. It should be independently reviewed before inclusion in a final manuscript. No new Lean execution is claimed.

# V3.47 — Breuil–Kisin frame height and closure under links

Pinned Lean snapshot: 7cbf7a0c32fca1badf05931879a37c3f8a2b03b4. Companion to V3.45–V3.46. This document compresses the height adjustment, Witt-vector saturation, integrality/crystalline descent and de Rham link argument. It also isolates the remaining deep construction of the linked preframe. The algebraic arguments below are alternative natural-language proofs or condensed versions of the source, not a new Lean build or an independent reconstruction of all p-adic Hodge theory.

## 1. Exact frame data and quantifiers

Use the source's BK K package, write S=bk.S, E=bk.E, Ainf=W(O_C^flat), W=WCb=W(C^flat), i:Ainf→W, e=bk.ev:S→Ainf and e_W=i∘e. Let ξ=e(E), x=e(X). Here X is the formal variable of S. The Galois actions and Frobenius are those of the package, not arbitrary endomorphisms.

A preframe of height N for a d-dimensional integral representation ρ consists of
    T∈GL_d(W), A,A'∈M_d(S),
    AA'=E^(2N) I,
    e_W(E)^N φ(T)=T e_W(A),
    ρ(g) g(T)=T for g in FixFlat.
A frame adds ε_g∈M_d(Ainf) satisfying
    ρ(g)g(T)=T i(ε_g).
All integral matrices are embedded in W when used there.

IsCr means ε_g≡I mod x Ainf for all g.
IsDR means that for each integer M≥0 there exist k≥0 and β∈M_d(S) such that
    β≡ℓ^k I mod E,
    φ(ε_g) g(e(β))≡e(β) mod ξ^M Ainf for all g.
The same β works for every g. These are frame predicates; their connection to actual crystalline and de Rham representations uses the separate comparison theorems, not just the definitions.

A Link at precision n between target T and layer T_m with map Π_m provides R∈M_(d×d_m)(S) and G∈M_(d_m×d)(S) such that
    Π_m T_m≡T e_W(R) mod ℓ^n W,
    RG≡ℓ^r I mod ℓ^n S.
The single exponent r is independent of n, and m≥n can depend on n. The tower also gives Π_m ρ_m(g)≡ρ(g)Π_m mod ℓ^m. No exact right inverse of R is assumed.

## 2. From semistability to one common height

The source exists_frame_sst has the following proof:
1. Semistability implies de Rham; choose a Hodge basis H with |H.p_i−H.q|≤N from the two filtration bounds DdR⊂Fil^(−N), DdR∩Fil^(N+1)=0.
2. Construct WSeed using either crystalline integral period data (where κ_g=I), or semistable Acris period data and semistableSeed_of_acris_periods.
3. semistablePeriodFrame builds some frame at height N', through the period-basis construction and the integral Galois-stability theorem. Its IsDR holds and κ_g=I implies IsCr.
4. Adjust height N' to the prescribed N without changing T or ε_g.

The adjustment can be written as one algebraic operation. If N≥N', multiply both A,A' by E^(N−N'). If N<N', the adapted-basis pole theorem proves both matrices divisible entrywise by E^(N'−N); divide both by this power. The key identity is
    (E^k A)(E^k A')=E^(2k) AA',
and the Frobenius equation scales by e_W(E)^k. For lowering, cancel the same nonzero powers in the domains S and W. T, its inverse, the fixed-subgroup equation and the cocycle ε_g remain unchanged. In particular IsDR and IsCr are unchanged, since their defining formulas involve ε_g but not N or A,A'.

The difficult assertion is the divisibility in the lowering direction; it is not a formal consequence of the matrix product AA' alone. Source exists_frame_of_jumps_le obtains it from breuilKisinPreframe_bounded_poles and adaptedBasis_theta_galoisFixed_poles. This is the essential height-reduction input. Nor does this argument bound the preliminary WSeed denominator exponents. The common height comes from the prescribed jumps, not from uniform bounds on seed denominators.

## 3. A direct Witt-vector saturation argument

The key closure lemma states: if i(α) is a unit of W, r≥0 is fixed, and for every n
    ℓ^r y ≡ i(α a_n) mod ℓ^n W, a_n∈Ainf,
then y∈i(α Ainf).

For α=1, fix a Witt coordinate j. Use n=j+r+1. Congruence modulo ℓ^n implies equality of the first n Witt coordinates. In characteristic ℓ the (j+r)-th Witt coordinate of ℓ^r y is (y_j)^(ℓ^r). Therefore (y_j)^(ℓ^r)∈O_C^flat. Since this valuation ring is perfect and embeds in its fraction field, y_j∈O_C^flat (take the unique ℓ^r-th root). This holds for every j, so y∈W(O_C^flat).

For general α, multiply every congruence by i(α)^(-1), apply the first case, and multiply back. The inverse is in W by the hypothesis. This proves the exact statement of AinfSat.mem_ideal_of_saturated; it does not assume that α is a unit in Ainf. The source applies it with α=1 and α=x=e(X), whose image in W is a unit by the nonzero flat Teichmüller coordinate.

## 4. Unified cocycle descent and crystalline closure via quotient matrices

Suppose a target preframe T and linked layer frames are given as in Section 1. Define the unique possible target cocycle in W:
    D_g=T^(-1)ρ(g)g(T).
For a fixed n choose a layer m and witnesses R,G. Work first modulo ℓ^n W. Put A_R=e_W(R), B_G=e_W(G). By the link, its Galois transform, tower equivariance and the layer cocycle identity,
    T D_g g(A_R)
       = ρ(g)g(T)g(A_R)
       = ρ(g)Π_m g(T_m)
       = Π_m ρ_m(g)g(T_m)
       = Π_m T_m i(ε_(m,g))
       = T A_R i(ε_(m,g)).
Here equalities are in the quotient; Π_m is fixed by Galois because its coefficients are ℓ-adic integers. T remains invertible there, so
    D_g g(A_R)=A_R i(ε_(m,g)).
Right-multiply by g(B_G) and use RG≡ℓ^r I:
    ℓ^r D_g≡i(e(R) ε_(m,g) g(e(G))) mod ℓ^n W.
Apply Section 3 entrywise with α=1. It follows that D_g has integral Ainf entries; these unique entries give ε_(0,g), so the target preframe extends to a frame with the same preframe data.

For crystalline transfer, suppose every layer has ε_(m,g)≡I mod x. Galois acts trivially on e(S) modulo x: write s=Xt+c with c constant, use the fixed unramified coefficients and x | g(x). Thus g(e(G))≡e(G) mod x. In the preceding congruence the right-hand matrix consequently differs from e(RG) by an element of x M(Ainf), and e(RG)≡ℓ^r I mod ℓ^n Ainf. Therefore
    ℓ^r(D_g−I) ≡ i(x Z_(n,g)) mod ℓ^n W
for some integral matrix Z_(n,g). Section 3 with α=x gives D_g−I∈i(x M_d(Ainf)), exactly IsCr of the target.

This single quotient computation replaces the separate expansion into four error matrices in BreuilKisinFrameIsCrOfLinkedTower. It also explains why no uniform bounds on R or G are required, but a uniform denominator ℓ^r IS required. It proves frame_of_links and crystalline inheritance simultaneously, under the same Link hypotheses. It cannot simply cancel ℓ^r in the finite quotient, where ℓ is a zero divisor; saturation is the correct replacement.

For later use, once both cocycles are integral, the congruence
    ε_(0,g) g(e(R))≡e(R) ε_(m,g) mod ℓ^n Ainf
is justified by Witt coefficient equality and injectivity of the induced map modulo ℓ^n: both perfect characteristic-ℓ base rings give the usual truncation description. This is also the source's separate cocycles_linked theorem.

## 5. De Rham closure as finite-dimensional compactness

Fix the desired order M, and let q=M+1. Two source inputs are indispensable:
- beta_bdd_denominators supplies one c=c(q) such that any layer IsBeta(q,k,β) can be replaced by IsBeta(q,c,β') regardless of rank and original k.
- exists_inv_lift supplies one k0=k0(q) such that for every s∈S one can choose γ(s)∈S with γ(s)≡ℓ^k0 s mod E and g(e(γ(s)))≡e(γ(s)) mod ξ^q for ALL g.

For each n choose m,R,G as above, choose β' from the layer at order q with exponent c, and form the rectangular matrix Γ entrywise by
    Γ_ij=γ(φ_S(G_ij)).
Define one square target candidate
    β_n=φ_S(R) β' Γ ∈ M_d(S).
Its size d is fixed even though d_m varies.

Modulo (ℓ^n,E) in S, β'≡ℓ^c I, Γ≡ℓ^k0 φ_S(G) and φ_S(RG)≡ℓ^r I, so
    β_n≡ℓ^(c+k0+r) I mod (ℓ^n,E).                     (1)

Let B_R=e(R). Modulo (ℓ^n,ξ^q) in Ainf, apply Frobenius to the cocycle congruence from Section 4. Since it commutes with Galois and e intertwines φ_S with φ, we have
    φ(ε_0) g(φ(B_R))=φ(B_R) φ(ε_m).
Together with IsBeta of β' and invariance of e(Γ), this gives the single multiplication
    φ(ε_0) g(e(β_n))
      =φ(B_R) φ(ε_m) g(e(β')) g(e(Γ))
      =φ(B_R) e(β') e(Γ)
      =e(β_n).                                           (2)
Again this is a quotient equality, not exact before taking the limit.

Let Q_n(β) denote (1) and (2) for every g. Then Q_(n+1)⇒Q_n; each Q_n is nonempty; and membership depends only on β modulo (ℓ^n,E^q) because e and Galois preserve the relevant ideals and the matrix expression is linear in β. Apply the source's compactness theorem for the finite-rank quotient S/E^q (equivalently the nested congruence predicates on its finite-coordinate ℓ-adic compact space). This gives ONE β satisfying every Q_n, not a separate choice of β for each g.

The source's Eisenstein ideal properties give closedness of ξ^q Ainf and reflection of E-divisibility through e. Consequently (1) becomes β≡ℓ^(c+k0+r) I mod E, and (2) becomes φ(ε_0)g(e(β))≡e(β) mod ξ^q. Since q=M+1≥M, these are the exact IsBeta requirements at order M. As M was arbitrary, the target is IsDR.

In particular one must not merely take a pointwise limit of the original β' matrices: their dimensions vary, and their original k may diverge. The uniform denominator normalization, invariant lift, and fixed-size target matrix are what make the compactness argument possible.

## 6. The remaining existence bottleneck: linked preframe

The top-level limit_frame_exists uses four steps: limit_pre_exists; frame_of_links; isDR_of_links; crystalline closure. Sections 3–5 condense the last three steps given the linked preframe. The first step is a separate substantial construction. The full source ExistsBreuilKisinBasisLimitLattice.lean supplies the following dependency chain:
1. exists_etale_basis_limit_lattice_tower: obtain an étale basis T0 and compatible layer coefficient data.
2. limit_breuilKisin_basis_invariant_etale: establish fixed subgroup and Frobenius data over the étale ring.
3. approximatingLattices_transitionMatrix_modPow, latticeApproximation_mittagLeffler, mittagLeffler_image_breuilKisin_modPow: construct limiting images.
4. limitVector_mem_OE_and_ainf and limitVectors_invertible_basis_over_OE: get integral membership and a basis over OE.
5. saturatedAinfLattice_free_over_breuilKisin: obtain a free saturated lattice and adjusted basis T0 Wm.
6. twisted_matrix_congruence_lift_sequence, ainf_exists_mul_eq_pow_smul_of_mod_lPow: obtain bounded-pole inverse data by compatible approximation.
7. invariantEtaleBasis_change_by_OE_unit and breuilKisin_basis_of_etale_invariant_bounded_poles: produce actual preframe P.
8. exists_right_inverse_mod_pow_of_ev together with ainf_rightInverse_up_to_pow_mod_lPow: lift the approximate right inverse to S with fixed exponent r and produce Link for all precisions.

This chain is not independently proved by Sections 3–5. The next target is the Mittag-Leffler/saturated lattice part and uniform right-inverse lift. The semistable period seed and adapted-basis height-divisibility inputs also remain to be expanded. Once these are supplied, the condensed closure arguments above finish the frame part of V3.45 without a new assumption of ordinary convergence.

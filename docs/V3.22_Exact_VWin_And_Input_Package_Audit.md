## 1. Why VWin deserves a separate audit

The final RH route passes through the stronger statement EveryCharacter, and the construction of EveryCharacter consumes VWin together with Green regular-character data and a definite-side hypothesis package.

VWin is not a small technical lemma. Its formal definition is a uniform family of arithmetic estimates involving number fields, cyclotomic extensions, Galois cohomology data, and Sha lengths. Its exact quantifiers matter: in particular, VWinAt is conditional on a pair of primes satisfying WinPair; it does not, by itself, assert that such a pair exists.

## 2. Exact formal content of VWin

The upstream definitions are in Development/AutomorphicArithmetic/CharacterBoxes/ExistsPageN.lean.

For a number field E, VVle E t B means:

1. For every algebraic closure Ω of E and every family of Galois-cohomology data ld indexed by rational primes p, the Sha length at every exponent e with 0 < e < t is finite.
2. For every finite set S of rational primes,
   
   \[
   \sum_{\ell\in S}\log \ell
   \sum_{e=1}^{t-1}\operatorname{shaLen}(ld_\ell,e)
   \le B.
   \]

The second clause is a uniform weighted-sum bound. The first clause is not implied merely by writing down the second inequality: the definition explicitly requires finiteness separately.

The field condition IsChiField ψ e q d E requires an embedding of E into a cyclotomic field of conductor N, with:

- \(2^{e+2}\mid N\), \(\psi.N\mid N\), \(q\mid N\), and \(d\mid N\);
- a primitive N-th root of unity;
- a precise equivalence between the fixed field of each cyclotomic automorphism and four arithmetic conditions: a pair of congruences modulo \(2^{e+2}\), the character condition \(\psi(a)=1\), and two Jacobi-symbol conditions involving q and d.

For x and eps, VAt ψ eps Ct q d e x universally quantifies over all number fields E satisfying IsChiField and requires:

\[
\operatorname{VVle}\left(E,\lfloor x^{Ct}\rfloor,
\sqrt{\exp(x^\varepsilon)}\right).
\]

The PageN condition chooses e(x) so that:

\[
x^{2+8\varepsilon}\le 2^{e(x)}
\le 2x^{2+8\varepsilon}
\qquad (x\ge1).
\]

Finally, VWinAt ψ Ct says that for every \(0<\varepsilon<1/52\), every such exponent selector e, and every sufficiently large X, there are J≥1 and X0 such that for every X≥X0 and every pair (q,d) satisfying WinPair at scale \(X^{1/J}\), one can find Y with

\[
X^{1/J}\le Y,\qquad Y^2\le X,
\]

and the VAt estimate holds for every x throughout the entire interval \([Y,Y^2]\).

The global statement is:

\[
\texttt{VWin}:=\forall\psi:\texttt{PChar},\quad
\texttt{VWinAt}(\psi,401).
\]

Thus the exponent 401 is fixed in the theorem, while the error exponent eps varies in the interval \((0,1/52)\).

## 3. The prime-pair condition is separate

WinPair eps Y q d requires:

- q and d prime;
- q ≡ 3 mod 8 and d ≡ 7 mod 16;
- the logarithm of q lies in a prescribed interval of scale \(Y^{\varepsilon/5}\);
- the logarithm of d lies in a prescribed interval of scale \(Y^{\varepsilon/5}(\log Y)^2\);
- q divides d+1;
- every odd prime p up to \(Y^{\varepsilon/5}\) divides d+1.

VWinAt only says that the Sha estimate holds for every pair satisfying this predicate. Existence of suitable pairs is handled by a separate theorem, WinPairs.winPairs, which also uses a PairEx input. This distinction is important when interpreting the strength and role of VWin.

## 4. How the VWin input is assembled

The theorem UpperTheory.One.vwin_of_row takes a Galois row h and constructs VWin from:

- OpenImageCondition.mk h;
- BaseChangeIdentities.baseChangeN;
- the same Galois row h.

The name OpenImageCondition should not be read as a separate open-image hypothesis in the usual informal sense. In the pinned source it is a proposition structure with one field, galoisRepOfPolarizedPairLit : RowT. RowT is the Galois representation row type.

The intermediate LiteratureRankNHypothesis is a four-field structure:

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| loc | Local representation-theory inputs |
| aut | Automorphic and Galois inputs |
| coh | Galois-cohomology inputs |
| cm | CM and abelian-variety inputs |

The source constructs these four fields explicitly in TopMainZj.lean and CorGKOf.lean:

- loc is assembled by local_inputs_from_literature;
- aut is assembled by automorphic_inputs_from_literature, using the base-change theorem and the Galois row;
- coh is assembled by galois_cohomology_from_literature;
- cm is assembled by cm_abelian_inputs_from_literature.

The VWin theorem then applies published_hypotheses_from_patching_alt, derives the intermediate HypothesesH' package, and invokes vwin_of_hypotheses_H_E_alt. That proof proceeds through the Kato/automorphy, local representation, unitary-lift, and patching/descent estimates before producing VWin.

## 5. The Galois row that feeds this construction

Row0b is a substantial universal statement. For every CM number field E, algebraic closure Ω, prime l, coefficient-field identification, dimension a, regular-algebraic cuspidal representation π, and polarized-pair character χ, it asserts the existence of a Galois representation r together with:

- continuity of r;
- semisimplicity;
- local compatibility away from l;
- the prescribed Hodge-Tate weight identity;
- conjugation compatibility of the weight data;
- crystalline and local compatibility properties above l.

The theorem galoisLocalGlobalStrong_recParameter obtains Row0b from:

\[

\]

The Tools_k structure is filled with explicit theorem results for the Siegel-Weil, doubling, compactness, theta-kernel, Lie-theoretic, and representation-theoretic components. The final lift theorem then assembles Rallis, decomposition, bump, datum, tail, Schur-step, automorphy-lift, projection, and read-lift components.

## 6. The final EveryCharacter construction consumes more than VWin

The final call in EveryCharacterOfLiteratureOfVWin.lean has the form:

\[
\texttt{every\_character\_of\_literature\_of\_vWin}(O,h_V).
\]

It constructs the following packages:

1. A unitary-group third-place hypothesis package from published_results_eigen O.
2. Theta local inputs from published_results_first O.
3. Theta-integral agreement and local matching from theoremK_head2.
4. A covolume bound from the arithmetic/intersection-theory chain.
5. Local Langlands RecT existence from the literature package.
6. The VWin input.

SiegelEisensteinSecondTermHypotheses.lean then constructs the definite-side hypothesis package, including row rationality, Fourier-coefficient fit, evaluation-grid bounds, open-parameter statements, linked-bundle bounds, and the unramified-exponent conclusion.

The final zero-free theorem combines:

- Green regular-character data;
- VWin, converted into host bounds by supplyChi;
- the constructed definite-side hypotheses.

The resulting zero-free statement is then passed through the Chebyshev-window and Landau/FormB route before primitive and imprimitive character reductions yield EveryCharacter.

## 7. Provenance caveat: theorem wrappers versus assumptions

Many declarations with names ending in of_open or of_literature are ordinary theorem wrappers whose proof terms directly reference other theorem declarations. For example, the Green regular-character wrapper returns the previously defined PositivityTheory.Stage1.PM.greenRegularCharacters result; the cusp rapid-decay wrapper returns GlobalTheory.RapidDecay.cuspRapidDecay.

This is not, by itself, evidence of an unproved assumption. It means the audit must follow the referenced theorem constants to their own definitions and transitive dependencies. The planned #print axioms run is necessary to determine the axiom closure of the final declarations; a source-level name or README scan is not enough.
